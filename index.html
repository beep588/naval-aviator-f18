MSFS 2024 FLIGHT BRIEFING & PROCEDURES

1. Taxi onto Bow Catapult 1. Lower Launch Bar.

2. Set Horizontal Stabilators to 16° nose-up for heavy gross weight.

3. Throttles to Full Military, check gauges, advance to full Afterburner.

4. Salute shooter, take hands off flight stick.

5. On stroke: let jet self-rotate; clean gear and flaps, establish 500 ft parallel departure.

Step 2: 3-Minute Free Hosting Setup (GitHub Pages)

  1. Log in to GitHub.com and click "New Repository".
  2. Name it something cool (e.g. naval-aviator-f18) and ensure it is set to Public.
  3. Click "Upload an existing file" and drag your downloaded index.html directly into the repo. Click Commit changes.
  4. Go to your repository's Settings tab → Click Pages on the left sidebar.
